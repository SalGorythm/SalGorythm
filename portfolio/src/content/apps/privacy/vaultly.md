**The short version.** Vaultly keeps your documents on your device. It does not show ads, and it does not upload your vault to our servers as part of normal use. A file leaves the app only when you choose to open or share it with another app.

## What Vaultly stores, and where

Everything below is stored **only on your device**:

| Data | Where |
|---|---|
| Document details: names, categories, pins | A local database on your phone |
| The PDFs and images you import | The app's private storage |
| Your passcode | Stored as a hash, never in plain text |
| Lock settings (biometrics, auto-lock) and Pro status | The device's secure store, where available |
| Preferences such as theme and emergency access | On your device |

Vaultly is offline-first and is not a cloud sync product. We (the developer) cannot see your documents or their details.

## Permissions Vaultly uses

- **Face ID / Touch ID** (optional): to unlock the vault. Biometric checks are handled by your device's operating system; Vaultly never receives your face or fingerprint data.
- **Files and photos picker:** only when you choose a PDF or image to add. Vaultly receives only the files you pick.

## When data leaves your device

Only when you do it — for example, opening or sharing a document through another app such as Mail or AirDrop. That app then handles the file under its own privacy policy.

## Purchases

Vaultly Pro is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Apple or Google through their stores. We don't receive your payment details.

## What we don't do

- No ads, inside the app or anywhere in your vault.
- No uploading of your vault to our servers.
- No selling or sharing of your documents or their details.

## Backups and deleting your data

Vaultly has no built-in cloud backup. If you lose or reset your device, your vault may be lost — keep originals or a backup you trust for anything you can't replace.

To delete your data, remove individual documents in the app, or delete the app to remove everything it stores.

## Changes

If the way Vaultly handles data changes, this policy will be updated at this address and the date above will change.
