This policy explains how Pickwheel ("the app") handles information. The short version: **we don't collect any data from you.** Everything you enter stays on your device.

## 1. Information we collect

**None.** Pickwheel has no accounts, no sign-up, no servers of its own, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party data-collection tools. We don't receive your names, groups, picks, or any information about how you use the app.

On the App Store, Pickwheel's privacy label is **"Data Not Collected"**.

## 2. Information stored on your device

To work, the app stores the following **only on your device**:

- **Groups and names** you create, type, paste or import.
- **Round state:** who has been drawn in the current round, and who is "away today".
- **History** of picks: the name, round, mode and time. The last 500 per group are kept.
- **Settings:** appearance, sound and haptics, the last mode used, team count, dice and number-range settings.
- **Pro status:** whether Pro has been purchased, kept in the iOS Keychain so the app works offline.

We can't see any of this. It's removed when you delete it in the app or delete the app. Like other app data, it may be included in your device's iCloud or computer backups, which are controlled by your Apple Account settings and Apple's privacy policy.

## 3. In-app purchases

Pro is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment. We never see your name, email address, Apple Account or payment details. The app asks Apple's StoreKit whether Pro has been purchased. This is the **only network connection** the app makes, and it goes to Apple. Apple gives developers sales reports that do not identify you. Apple's handling of your purchase is covered by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## 4. Files and the clipboard

- **Import:** when you choose to import, the system file picker opens and the app reads only the file you select, to add its names to a group.
- **Export:** the app creates a text or CSV file and opens the system share sheet. You decide where it goes, whether that's Files, AirDrop, Mail or somewhere else.
- **Clipboard:** the app writes to the clipboard only when you tap **Copy** in Order mode. It never reads your clipboard.

## 5. Permissions

Pickwheel does not ask for access to your camera, microphone, photos, contacts, location, calendar, health data or Face ID. It doesn't use Apple's tracking (ATT) prompt, because it doesn't track you.

## 6. Crash reports

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings (**Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements → Share With App Developers**), Apple may send us anonymous crash reports. These don't contain your groups or names, and you can turn sharing off at any time.

## 7. Children

Pickwheel is suitable for classrooms and families. Because the app doesn't collect any personal information from anyone, it doesn't knowingly collect personal information from children. This is consistent with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the UK and EU GDPR. Names typed into the app by a teacher or parent stay on that device.

## 8. Your rights

Data protection laws such as the GDPR, the UK GDPR and the CCPA give you rights to access, correct and delete your personal data. Since we don't hold any of your data, there is nothing for us to access or delete. You have full control in the app:

- **Correct:** rename any group or name.
- **Delete:** remove names, clear history, delete groups, or delete the app.
- **Export:** with Pro, export any group as a text or CSV file.

## 9. Security

Your data stays on your device and is protected by iOS, including data protection while your iPhone is locked. Your Pro status is kept in the iOS Keychain.

## 10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top and publish the new version on this page. If the app ever started collecting data, which we don't plan to do, we would say so here and in the App Store before it happened.
