**The short version.** WakeUp does not collect your data. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. What you put into WakeUp stays on your iPhone, and leaves it only when you choose to share or export it.

## What WakeUp stores, and where

All of the following is stored **only on your device**, inside WakeUp's own storage:

| Data | Why |
|---|---|
| Your alarms and their settings (time, days, mission, sound, snooze, Stay-Up Check) | To ring them |
| Morning records: alarm time, when you dismissed it, snoozes, mission result, morning steps | History and your streak |
| Scan-a-code missions: a one-way fingerprint (hash) of the code, not the code itself | To check you scanned the right code |
| Photo missions: the reference photo you take and a small fingerprint of it | To check the photo matches; never uploaded |
| Your signed commitment (the drawn signature) | Shown on your home screen and in the optional wind-down reminder |
| Sounds you add from Files (trimmed copy) | To play as your alarm |
| The background photo you choose (a resized copy) | Shown behind your alarm screen |
| Lines you write for the alarm screen | Shown as your line of the day |
| Preferences (theme, sounds, light, bedtime, faith pack, morning steps) | To remember your choices |
| Qibla mission: the direction angle only. Face-the-sunrise mission: your latitude rounded to about 11 km | To point you the right way |
| Pro purchase status (in the device's secure keychain) | So Pro works offline |

We (the developer) cannot see any of it. There is no copy on any server.

## Permissions WakeUp asks for

Each is optional and used only for the feature named:

- **Notifications:** to ring the alarm and, if you turn it on, the wind-down reminder.
- **iOS Alarms (AlarmKit):** so the alarm can ring through silent mode and Focus.
- **Camera:** only during the scan-a-code and photo missions and their setup. Images are processed on the device. The photo mission keeps only your reference photo, on the device.
- **Motion & Fitness:** to count steps during a Steps mission. Step counts are not stored.
- **Location (While Using):** once, when you set up the Qibla or face-the-sunrise mission, to work out a direction. The location itself is not stored or sent anywhere.
- **Files:** only when you pick a sound to add.
- **Photos:** none. Choosing a background uses Apple's photo picker, which gives WakeUp only the single photo you pick, with no access to your library. A resized copy stays on the device.
- **Live Activities and widgets:** to show your next alarm on the lock screen and home screen. They read the same on-device data.

## Purchases

WakeUp Pro is a one-time in-app purchase processed by **Apple** through the App Store. We don't receive your payment details or Apple Account information. The app checks with Apple, on the device, that the purchase is valid. Apple's handling of purchases is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

## When data leaves your device

Only when you do it:

- **Export as CSV**, which opens the iOS share sheet; you choose where it goes.
- **Text a friend** (sound unlock), which opens the share sheet with a message you can edit.
- **Print a code sheet**, which uses iOS printing.

**Backups:** like all apps, WakeUp's data may be included in your iCloud or computer backups, according to your own iOS settings. That's managed by Apple and by you.

## What we don't do

- No analytics, no crash-reporting SDKs, no advertising, no tracking across apps or websites, no data brokers, no selling or sharing of personal data.
- No account, no sign-in, no email collection inside the app.
- No network requests from WakeUp itself, other than Apple's App Store services for the purchase.

## Your choices and rights

- **See and export** everything WakeUp has recorded: History → Export as CSV.
- **Delete:** remove individual alarms, sounds, photos or mission codes in the app, or delete the app to remove everything. There is no server copy to request deletion of.
- **Withdraw permissions** at any time in iOS Settings → WakeUp.

Because we don't collect or hold your personal data, requests under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA (access, correction, deletion, portability) are fulfilled by the tools above, on your device. You can still contact us with any question.

## Children

WakeUp is rated 4+ and does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

## Health and safety

WakeUp is not a medical device. The wake light and sunrise ramp brighten your screen; they are not light therapy and don't treat any condition.

## Changes

If WakeUp ever starts collecting any data, this policy and the App Store privacy label will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
