## 1. Information we collect

EasySalah does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

We do not require:

- Account creation
- Login
- Email address
- Phone number

We do not collect:

- Personal information
- Usage analytics
- Tracking data
- Advertising identifiers

## 2. Location information

EasySalah may request access to your device's location only to:

- Calculate accurate prayer times
- Show Qibla direction
- Provide local Islamic date information

Your location data:

- Is processed locally on your device
- Is never stored
- Is never sent to any server
- Is never shared with third parties

You can disable location access anytime in your device settings.

## 3. Data storage

All app settings and preferences are stored locally on your device. We do not operate any backend servers for storing user data.

## 4. Third-party services

EasySalah does not use:

- Third-party analytics
- Advertising networks
- Tracking tools

**Pro Tools (optional):** one-time purchases and restores are processed through Apple or Google using their standard in-app purchase APIs. EasySalah does not operate a custom payment server inside the app for this entitlement.

If this changes in future versions, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

## 5. Children's privacy

EasySalah is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

## 6. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
