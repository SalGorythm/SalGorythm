**What it does:** WakeUp rings, then asks you for something small that gets you up: solve a
few sums, scan a code you've stuck in the kitchen, walk thirty steps, match a photo, or read a
line from your faith pack. Once you're up, it walks you through a short morning sequence you
chose the night before: water, curtains, ten pushups, a look at today. One tap each.

**The three moments:** **Night** (set it) → **Dawn** (get up) → **Morning** (start).

**The promise:** it works for you, it never traps you, it never shames you, and nothing leaves
your phone.

## 2. Who it's for

- **People who snooze through alarms** and want an alarm that gets them out of bed, not one
  they turn off in their sleep.
- **People building a morning routine** who want the first ten minutes of the day to go
  the same way each time, without another habit app.
- **People of faith** who want their morning to begin with a reading, a dhikr or japa count, a
  prayer, Fajr (Qibla), or by facing the sunrise, with sources shown and nothing scored.
- **Privacy-minded people** tired of subscriptions, accounts and apps that track them.
- **People who've been let down by alarm apps** and want to know, honestly, whether tonight's
  alarm will ring.

## 3. Positioning and differentiators

**Category:** mission alarm and morning routine.

**What makes WakeUp different:**

1. **Morning, not just alarm.** Most mission alarms stop at "you're awake". WakeUp continues
   into a short morning sequence and Morning Plan, so getting up leads somewhere.
2. **An exit hatch, always.** "Dismiss anyway" appears after 3 tries or 90 seconds. You can
   never be trapped by a ringing phone.
3. **No shame.** No red for missed mornings, no broken-streak animation. A missed day is just
   a gap.
4. **Honest about iOS.** A pre-flight check shows "Armed and audible", or the exact reason
   tonight's alarm might not ring, with a one-tap fix. It never claims more than iOS allows.
5. **Faith packs done with care.** Islamic, Hindu, Christian and Secular. Every text is sourced
   and reviewed. Tap-through only: never voice-checked, never scored.
6. **Private by design.** No account, no server, no analytics SDK. Nothing leaves the device.
7. **Pay once.** One-time Pro. No subscription, no ads, no upsell pop-ups. The core job (an
   alarm that rings and a mission that turns it off) is free.

## 4. How a morning works

**Night: set it**
- The home screen is your **Morning Plan**: tomorrow's alarm, a live countdown, your mission
  and your morning steps on one screen.
- **Bedtime** is worked out for you: "For 7h30, be asleep by 11:00 PM." An optional
  **wind-down reminder** nudges you before then.
- Sign a short **commitment** to yourself. It appears on your home screen.
- The **pre-flight check** tells you, in green, amber or red, whether the alarm will be heard.
- Put the phone on its charger in **Nightstand** mode: a dim bedside clock. If you turned on
  the **sunrise ramp** (Pro), the screen warms slowly before the alarm.

**Dawn: get up**
- The alarm rings. Using iOS alarms (iOS 26.1+) it rings through silent mode and Focus, with
  a backup chain of time-sensitive notifications.
- Open it and you see a simple **two-step alarm screen**: the time, one big button to start
  your mission, and "Snooze · N left". Optional: your **background photo** and a
  **line of the day**.
- The **wake light** brightens the screen gently and lights your way.
- Do the mission. If it isn't working, **"Dismiss anyway"** appears after 3 tries or 90 seconds.
- **Snooze** if you need to: each snooze is a little shorter, and you choose how many you get.
- **Stay-Up Check** (optional): a few minutes later the alarm rings once more, unless you've
  already ticked a morning step or tapped "I'm up". It rings at most twice.

**Morning: start**
- Your **morning sequence**: the 2 to 5 steps you chose. One tap each.
- Finish it and the morning counts towards your **streak**. Miss a day and it's simply a gap.
- **History** shows your mornings by week, with a free CSV export.

## 5. Every feature, in detail

### 5.1 Alarms
- Set the time with a clear time picker; choose repeat days (or one-off).
- Each alarm has a **mission**, a **sound**, **snooze** settings and an optional
  **Stay-Up Check**.
- **Test alarm**: rings in 10 seconds, so you can check it with your phone set up the way
  it'll be in the morning.
- Alarms are scheduled with iOS, so they ring even if the app is closed or force-quit.
- Free: 1 alarm. Pro: as many as you want.

### 5.2 Missions (how you turn the alarm off)

| Mission | What you do | Group | Tier |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Math** | Solve a few sums (easy, medium or hard; choose how many) | Wake the mind | Free |
| **Scan a code** | Scan a barcode or QR code you've placed somewhere else (e.g. the kettle). WakeUp can print a code sheet for you. | Get out of bed | Pro |
| **Photo match** | Take a photo matching a reference photo you set up (e.g. your sink) | Get out of bed | Pro |
| **Steps** | Walk a number of steps (counted by your iPhone's motion sensor) | Get out of bed | Pro |
| **Retype a line** | Type a line exactly as shown | Wake the mind | Pro |
| **Gratitude line** | Write one thing you're grateful for | Gentle | Faith pack* |
| **Daily reading** | Read a short sourced text from your faith pack, then tap through | Faith | Faith pack* |
| **Count** | Tasbīh (Islamic), Japa (Hindu) or a prayer count (Christian): tap to count | Faith | Faith pack* |
| **Qibla** | Hold your phone facing the Qibla for a few seconds | Faith (Islamic) | Faith pack* |
| **Face the sunrise** | Turn to face where the sun rises | Faith | Faith pack* |

\* Faith missions are **free in your first chosen tradition**. Other packs need Pro.

- **"Dismiss anyway"**: appears after 3 failed tries or 90 seconds on any mission.
- Leaving the app mid-mission makes the alarm ring again a few seconds later.
- Scan-a-code stores only a one-way fingerprint (hash) of the code. Photo match stores your
  reference photo and a small fingerprint, on the phone only.

### 5.3 The alarm screen
- **Two steps**: the time and one big mission button, then the mission. Nothing to fumble
  with half-asleep.
- **Wake light**: the screen brightens gently through warm colours.
- **Line of the day**: a short public-domain quote (sourced), your faith pack's reading, or
  **lines you write yourself**.
- **Background photo** (Pro): a photo you choose, behind a soft scrim so text stays readable.
  People use family photos, a goal, someone who inspires them.
- **Moon button**: quick access to the night view.

### 5.4 Snooze
- On by default, with a limit you choose (shown as "Snooze · N left").
- Each snooze is a little shorter than the last (never below 3 minutes).

### 5.5 Stay-Up Check
- Optional, per alarm. WakeUp explains it before you turn it on.
- A few minutes after your mission, the alarm rings once more, unless you've ticked a morning
  step or tapped "I'm up — stop checking".
- Rings at most twice. Never more.

### 5.6 Morning sequence and Morning Plan
Choose 2 to 5 steps from 15:

| Step | Hint |
|---|---|
| Drink a glass of water | Takes 30 seconds |
| Rinse your mouth | Or brush your teeth |
| Splash your face | Cold water wakes you up fast |
| Open the curtains | Let the daylight in |
| Get some fresh air | Balcony, window or doorstep |
| Ten pushups | Or whatever you manage |
| Twenty jumping jacks | Gets the blood moving |
| Stretch for a minute | Reach up, then touch your toes |
| Take a short walk | Five minutes is plenty |
| Make your bed | One thing done already |
| Five slow breaths | In for four, out for six |
| Morning reading | From your chosen pack |
| Make wudu | Ready for Fajr *(Islamic pack only)* |
| Pick today's top three | The three things that matter most |
| Look at today | What is ahead of you |

- The **Morning Plan** on the home screen shows tomorrow as a timeline: alarm → mission →
  steps.
- **Streak** counts completed mornings. Free. No shame: no red, no broken-streak animation.

### 5.7 Evening
- **Bedtime**: set how much sleep you want; WakeUp tells you when to be asleep.
- **Wind-down reminder** (optional notification).
- **Signed commitment**: draw your signature under a short promise to yourself.
- **Nightstand**: a dim, burn-in-safe bedside clock for your charger. Keeps the screen on at
  very low brightness.
- **Sunrise ramp** (Pro): in Nightstand, the screen warms from deep red to soft daylight before
  the alarm. Respects Reduce Motion (it jumps straight to the end state).

### 5.8 Pre-flight readiness check
A card at the top of the home screen:
- **Green: "Armed and audible."**
- **Amber or red**: the exact reason (notifications off, sounds off, Scheduled Summary,
  Focus blocking time-sensitive alerts, silent mode without iOS alarms, Low Power Mode), each
  with a one-tap fix or clear instructions.

### 5.9 Sounds
- **Free:** WakeUp tone, Soft rise, and **iPhone ringtone** (whatever ringtone you've set in
  iPhone Settings).
- **Pro:** Morning chimes, Bell tower, Pulse, Marimba.
- **Dawn chorus:** unlocked free by texting one friend about WakeUp (optional; you see and
  edit the message).
- **Your own sound** (Pro): pick any audio file from Files. WakeUp keeps the first 29 seconds
  (an iOS limit for alarm sounds). Copy-protected Apple Music songs can't be used.
- **Gradual volume** and **vibration** (off / gentle / strong) on the alarm screen.

### 5.10 History, widgets, Live Activity
- **History**: your mornings by week (completed, missed, no alarm that day), rolling stats,
  and **Export as CSV**. Free.
- **Widgets** (home screen and lock screen): next alarm, armed status.
- **Live Activity**: shows the alarm is armed on the lock screen and Dynamic Island.

### 5.11 Faith packs
One at a time, switchable. Your first choice is free forever; others need Pro.

| Pack | Includes |
|---|---|
| **Islamic** | Du'ā on waking and other morning du'ās, Tasbīh count, Qibla mission, wudu step |
| **Hindu** | Gītā ślokas, Karadarśanam on waking, Bhūmi vandanam, Japa mantra count |
| **Christian** | A morning verse, short morning prayers, the Jesus Prayer count |
| **Secular** | Affirmation, gratitude, an intention for the day |

- Every text shows its **source** and is **reviewed** before release.
- Missions are **tap-through only**: never voice-checked, never scored.
- A mission dismisses an alarm. It is never presented as an act of worship.

### 5.12 Accessibility
- Full Dynamic Type, VoiceOver labels on every control.
- Respects Reduce Motion (ramps and animations cut straight to the end state).
- Steady vibration patterns only; no flashing or strobing.
- Light and dark themes.

### 5.13 Onboarding
A short setup: your wake time, a mission, your morning steps, optional faith pack, and the
permissions explained one by one before iOS asks. A test alarm is offered at the end.

## 7. Privacy, in plain words

- **No account.** Nothing to sign up for.
- **No server.** WakeUp has no backend. Your data has nowhere to go.
- **No analytics, no crash-reporting SDK, no ads, no tracking.**
- **Everything stays on your iPhone**: alarms, history, photos, sounds, signature.
- **Data leaves only when you choose**: Export as CSV, Text a friend, or Print a code sheet,
  each through the standard iOS share or print sheet.
- **Permissions are optional and narrow**: each one is used only for the feature that needs it.
- **No Photos library access**: the background picker gives WakeUp only the one photo you pick.
- **Location** is used once, to work out a direction (Qibla or sunrise), and isn't stored.
- **The purchase** goes through Apple; WakeUp never sees payment details.
- App Store privacy label: **Data Not Collected.**

## 8. Reliability: what iOS allows

WakeUp uses two layers so the alarm rings when the app is closed:

1. **iOS alarm (iOS 26.1 and later)**: a real alarm, the same kind the Clock app uses. Apple
   says these ring on silent and in a Focus. You allow it once.
2. **Notification chain**: a series of time-sensitive notifications with the alarm sound,
   repeated until you open the app. These get through most Focus modes. A background refresh
   tops them up so future alarms stay scheduled.

**What can stop an alarm** (on any iPhone):
- Silent mode, if iOS alarms aren't allowed for WakeUp.
- Low ringer volume (notification sounds use ringer volume).
- A Focus that blocks time-sensitive notifications, or notifications turned off for WakeUp.
- An Apple Watch on your wrist while the iPhone is locked (alerts may go to the watch).
- Headphones or a Bluetooth speaker.
- A phone that's off or out of battery.
- Deleting the app.

**What doesn't stop it:** closing or force-quitting the app; swiping a notification away.

**The advice we always give:** try a test alarm tonight, with your phone set the way it'll be
in the morning.

## 9. Brand, voice and design

### Voice
- **Plain, warm, honest.** Short sentences. Talk like a friend who gets up early.
- **Second person** ("you"), present tense.
- **Kind.** Never guilt, never shame, never "you failed".
- **Precise.** Say what it does and what it can't. Numbers only when they're real.
- **British/neutral spelling** in the app ("colour", "practise" as verb); keep it consistent.

**Sounds like WakeUp:** "One thing done already." · "You'll never be stuck with a ringing
phone." · "A missed day is just a gap." · "Armed and audible."

**Doesn't:** "Crush your mornings!" · "Never oversleep again — guaranteed!" · "Join 1M early
risers" · "Last chance: 50% off".

### Visual identity: night → dawn → morning

| Token | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Night background | `#0C0F1A` (glow `#2C336B` at top) | Hero, dark sections |
| Dawn gradient | `#2A2140 → #6B3F52 → #C2713F` | Hero band, "how it rings" |
| Morning paper | `#FDF6EB → #F6EEE1` | Light sections, legal pages |

| Accent | `#E79A5F` (dark) / `#A64F1B` (light) | Buttons, links |

- **Fonts:** Bricolage Grotesque 800/600 (headings), Figtree 400/600 (body), IBM Plex Mono 500

## 10. Claims: what we say and never say

**Safe to say**
- "An alarm you turn off by getting up."
- "Rings on silent and in Focus with iOS alarms (iOS 26.1 and later)."
- "Rings even if you close the app."
- "Never stuck: 'Dismiss anyway' after 3 tries or 90 seconds."
- "No account. No ads. No tracking. Nothing leaves your iPhone."
- "Pay once. No subscription."
- "A gentle wake light" / "a sunrise on your screen".
- "Sourced, reviewed faith content."

**Never say**
- "Light therapy", "clinically proven", "resets your body clock", "beats SAD", or any health
  or medical claim.
- "Can't be skipped / silenced / deleted", "impossible to ignore", "guaranteed to wake you".
- Invented numbers: user counts, ratings, "X% wake up on time".
- Fake urgency: countdowns, "limited time", "price going up".
- That a mission is worship or that the app judges anyone's practice.
- Comparisons naming competitor apps.

**Always add, where health or light comes up:** "WakeUp is not a medical device. The wake
light and sunrise brighten your screen; they are not light therapy."

## 14. Support page and FAQ

**Contact:** `{{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}`. Plain email, no form. Expected reply: within 3 working days.

**My alarm didn't ring. What should I check?**
Open WakeUp and look at the card at the top of the home screen. It checks the things that can
silence an alarm and tells you which one applies: notifications or sounds off for WakeUp,
WakeUp in a Scheduled Summary, a Focus blocking time-sensitive notifications, silent mode
without iOS alarms allowed, Low Power Mode. Then run **Test alarm** with your phone set up the
way it will be in the morning.

**Does it ring when my iPhone is on silent?**
Yes, if you allow **iOS alarms** for WakeUp (iOS 26.1 or later). Without them, notification
sounds follow the silent switch, which is an iOS rule for all apps.

**Does it ring if I close or force-quit the app?**
Yes. Alarms are scheduled with iOS, not kept running in the app.

**Can I turn the alarm off without doing the mission?**
Yes. "Dismiss anyway" appears after 3 tries or 90 seconds. Being trapped by a ringing phone
helps nobody.

**What is the Stay-Up Check?**
If you turn it on, the alarm rings once more a few minutes after your mission, unless you tick
a morning step or tap "I'm up". It rings at most twice. You can switch it off per alarm.

**Why does the sunrise only work in Nightstand?**
iOS doesn't let a locked phone light its screen. Open Nightstand on a charger before bed, and
the screen warms up before your alarm.

**Can I use my iPhone ringtone?**
Yes. Choose "iPhone ringtone" in Sounds. The alarm notifications use your iPhone ringtone; on
the in-app alarm screen, WakeUp plays its own tone.

**Can I use my own sound?**
Yes, with Pro. Pick an audio file from the Files app; WakeUp keeps the first 29 seconds on
your phone. Songs from Apple Music are copy-protected and can't be used.

**Can I put my own photo or words on the alarm screen?**
Yes. Settings → Alarm screen. Your own lines are free; a background photo needs Pro.

**I bought Pro on another device.**
Settings → WakeUp Pro → Restore purchase, signed in with the same Apple Account.

**How do I get a refund?**
Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.

**How do I export or delete my data?**
History → **Export as CSV** gives you everything WakeUp has recorded. Deleting the app deletes
all its data; there's no copy anywhere else.

**Is the faith content accurate?**
Each text carries its source and is reviewed before it ships. If you spot an error, email
`{{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}` with the pack and the text, and it will be corrected.

**Is there an Android version?**
Not yet.

## 15. Privacy Policy (full text)

> **WakeUp Privacy Policy**
> Effective: 9 October 2026 · Developer: {{DEVELOPER_NAME}} · Contact: {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}

**The short version.** WakeUp does not collect your data. There is no account, no server, no
analytics, no advertising and no tracking. What you put into WakeUp stays on your iPhone, and
leaves it only when you choose to share or export it.

### What WakeUp stores, and where

All of the following is stored **only on your device**, inside WakeUp's own storage:

| Data | Why |
|---|---|
| Your alarms and their settings (time, days, mission, sound, snooze, Stay-Up Check) | To ring them |
| Morning records: alarm time, when you dismissed it, snoozes, mission result, morning steps | History and your streak |
| Scan-a-code missions: a one-way fingerprint (hash) of the code, not the code itself | To check you scanned the right code |
| Photo missions: the reference photo you take and a small fingerprint of it | To check the photo matches; never uploaded |
| Your signed commitment (the drawn signature) | Shown on your home screen and in the optional wind-down reminder |
| Sounds you add from Files (trimmed copy) | To play as your alarm |
| The background photo you choose (a resized copy) | Shown behind your alarm screen |
| Lines you write for the alarm screen | Shown as your line of the day |
| Preferences (theme, sounds, light, bedtime, faith pack, morning steps) | To remember your choices |
| Qibla mission: the direction angle only. Face-the-sunrise mission: your latitude rounded to about 11 km | To point you the right way |
| Pro purchase status (in the device's secure keychain) | So Pro works offline |

We (the developer) cannot see any of it. There is no copy on any server.

### Permissions WakeUp asks for

Each is optional and used only for the feature named:

- **Notifications:** to ring the alarm and, if you turn it on, the wind-down reminder.
- **iOS Alarms (AlarmKit):** so the alarm can ring through silent mode and Focus.
- **Camera:** only during the scan-a-code and photo missions and their setup. Images are
  processed on the device. The photo mission keeps only your reference photo, on the device.
- **Motion & Fitness:** to count steps during a Steps mission. Step counts are not stored.
- **Location (While Using):** once, when you set up the Qibla or face-the-sunrise mission, to
  work out a direction. The location itself is not stored or sent anywhere.
- **Files:** only when you pick a sound to add.
- **Photos:** none. Choosing a background uses Apple's photo picker, which gives WakeUp only the
  single photo you pick, with no access to your library. A resized copy stays on the device.
- **Live Activities and widgets:** to show your next alarm on the lock screen and home screen.
  They read the same on-device data.

### Purchases

WakeUp Pro is a one-time in-app purchase processed by **Apple** through the App Store. We don't
receive your payment details or Apple Account information. The app checks with Apple, on the
device, that the purchase is valid. Apple's handling of purchases is covered by Apple's
privacy policy.

### When data leaves your device

Only when you do it:
- **Export as CSV**, which opens the iOS share sheet; you choose where it goes.
- **Text a friend** (sound unlock), which opens the share sheet with a message you can edit.
- **Print a code sheet**, which uses iOS printing.

**Backups:** like all apps, WakeUp's data may be included in your iCloud or computer backups,
according to your own iOS settings. That's managed by Apple and by you.

### What we don't do

- No analytics, no crash-reporting SDKs, no advertising, no tracking across apps or websites,
  no data brokers, no selling or sharing of personal data.
- No account, no sign-in, no email collection inside the app.
- No network requests from WakeUp itself, other than Apple's App Store services for the
  purchase.

### Your choices and rights

- **See and export** everything WakeUp has recorded: History → Export as CSV.
- **Delete:** remove individual alarms, sounds, photos or mission codes in the app, or delete
  the app to remove everything. There is no server copy to request deletion of.
- **Withdraw permissions** at any time in iOS Settings → WakeUp.

Because we don't collect or hold your personal data, requests under laws such as the GDPR or
CCPA (access, correction, deletion, portability) are fulfilled by the tools above, on your
device. You can still contact us with any question.

WakeUp is rated 4+ and does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

### Health and safety

WakeUp is not a medical device. The wake light and sunrise ramp brighten your screen; they are
