## Pillars (highlight on homepage)

Use these as badges, a four-up row, or a short manifesto block.

### Always ad-free

Vaultly does not show ads. We are not building an attention economy inside your vault—your focus stays on your documents.

### Always local

Your document list and details live in a database **on your phone**. Files are copied into the app’s **private storage**. The app is **offline-first** and **not a cloud sync product** as shipped: your vault stays with you on the device.

### Privacy first

The app is built so **nothing is uploaded** by Vaultly unless **you** add that yourself later (for example, if you choose to share a file through another app). Your passcode is stored as a **hash**. Lock settings and Pro status use the device **secure store** when applicable.

### No clutter

The interface stays **minimal on purpose**: clear hierarchy, calm typography, and space—so Vaultly stays **easy to explain** and **easy to trust**.

## Trust & security (plain language)

**What stays on the device**

- Document **metadata** (names, categories, pins, etc.) in a **local database**.
- **Files** in the app’s **private folder** after you import them.

**What Vaultly does not do by design**

- It does **not** upload your vault to our servers as part of normal use (there is **no built-in cloud backup** in the product as described here—users who need backups should use a strategy they trust, such as encrypted exports or device backup, if/when you offer export).

**Authentication**

- Passcode is stored as a **hash**, not plaintext.
- Lock preferences and Pro entitlement use **platform secure storage** where implemented.

**If the user shares something**

- Opening or sharing a file through **another app** (Mail, AirDrop, etc.) is **their** choice—mention this honestly on the site if you add a “data you may send” subsection later.

## FAQ

**Is Vaultly free?**  
Yes. The free tier includes core vault features with a limit of **5 documents**. **Pro** removes the limit and adds **extra themes** for a **one-time $4.99** purchase (via the App Store when enabled).

**Does Vaultly show ads?**  
**No.** Ad-free is a core product decision—not an upsell.

**Where is my data stored?**  
On **your device**: local database for metadata and private app storage for files. See the in-app **Privacy** screen for the same plain-language summary.

**Do you upload my documents to the cloud?**  
The app is designed so **nothing is uploaded** by Vaultly unless **you** add that yourself later (for example, integrations or features you may ship in the future—keep this sentence in sync with the product).

**Is there a subscription?**  
**Pro is a one-time purchase**—not a subscription. (Store fees and taxes may apply per platform.)

**Can I recover my vault if I lose my phone?**  
Without a backup or export path you provide, a lost device can mean **lost data**—be honest on the site and consider documenting device backup or a future export feature when available.

**Who can I contact?**  
`YOUR_CONTACT_EMAIL` — or your contact form at `YOUR_CONTACT_URL`

**Do you collect data from children?**  
Vaultly is a **general-purpose document organizer**. If you do not knowingly market to children, say so clearly after legal review.
