## Home

### Pickwheel: Random Picker

**Spin, Draw Names, No Ads Ever.**

A random picker for groups such as a class, a team or a family. Spin a wheel, pull a name from a hat,
split everyone into teams, or shuffle a running order. Every pick is made with a cryptographic random
number generator. With fair mode on, nobody is picked twice until everyone has had a turn.

There are no ads, no accounts and no tracking, in the free version or in Pro.

[Download on the App Store]([APP STORE LINK])

### Why Pickwheel

**Made for a room full of people.** Most free spinner apps show ads, and a teacher can't put whatever an
ad network serves on a classroom wall. Pickwheel has never had ads and never will, so it's safe to
put on a projector in front of a class.

**Fair you can see.** With fair mode on, each name is drawn once per round. Drawn names stay on screen,
struck through, so the whole room can see it's fair. A round ends when everyone has been picked, and
the next one starts automatically.

**Honest randomness.** The result is chosen first, with your iPhone's cryptographic random number
generator, and the wheel is then animated to land on it. The animation never decides who wins, and the
name shown is always the one that was drawn.

**A wheel that feels real.** The wheel slows down naturally, the pointer flicks as each segment passes,
and every segment gives a soft click and a light tap you can feel.

## Features

### Six ways to pick

| Mode | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Wheel** | Spin to pick one name. Best when everyone is watching and the drama matters. |
| **Hat** | Pull a folded slip from a bowl. Two seconds instead of five, for when you just need an answer. |
| **Teams** | Split a group into balanced teams. Team sizes never differ by more than one, so 11 people make 4, 4 and 3, never 5, 5 and 1. |
| **Order** | Shuffle the group into a running order, revealed one line at a time, then copy it as text. Good for presentations, chores and turns. |
| **Dice & Coin** | Roll 1 to 6 dice with 4, 6, 8, 10, 12 or 20 sides, or flip a coin. |
| **Number** | Pick a number between any two values, with an optional "no repeats" setting. |

Switching modes never loses your list or your place in the round. The Wheel and the Hat share the same
round, so you can use either.

### Saved groups

Type or paste a list of names once, one per line or separated by commas, and it's saved. Class 7B, the
Monday standup, the family: each group keeps its own names, round and history. You can also import a
list from a text or CSV file and export it the same way.

### Fair mode

- **No repeats** until everyone in the group has been picked.
- **Visible pool:** drawn names stay on screen, struck through.
- **Put back:** tap a drawn name to put it back into the round.
- **Away today:** tap a name to leave someone out for the rest of the day. They come back automatically
  at midnight, even if you've changed time zones.
- **Remembered:** the round is saved, so closing the app or restarting your phone doesn't reset it.

### History

Every pick is recorded with its round and time. Each group has a history page showing draws round by
round and a count for every name, so you can see the picks are even. The last 500 picks per group are
kept, and you can clear a group's history at any time.

### Comfortable to use

- Light and dark appearance, following your iPhone or set by you.
- The tick sound follows the silent switch. Sound and haptics can each be turned off.
- With Reduce Motion on, every mode skips its animation and simply shows the result.
- VoiceOver labels on the wheel, the name pool and every control.
- Works on iPhone and iPad.

## Support

### Frequently asked questions

**Is it really random?**
Yes. Every pick uses your iPhone's cryptographic random number generator, the same kind used for
security, not a simple "random" function. The result is decided before any animation starts, and
results are spread evenly across every name.

**Why did the same person come up twice?**
Fair mode is probably off. Turn it on, and nobody is picked again until everyone in the group has been
picked.

**Someone is absent today. How do I leave them out?**
Tap their name under the wheel and choose **Away today**. They're left out until midnight, then
come back automatically. Tap again and choose **Back today** if they turn up after all.

**I picked someone by mistake. Can I undo it?**
In fair mode, tap the drawn name and choose **Put back**. They go back into the current round. The
pick stays in history, which is a record of what happened.

**How do I enter a whole class quickly?**
Create a group and paste the list, one name per line or separated by commas. With Pro, you can also
import a text or CSV file. In a CSV, the first column is used.

**How do I restore my Pro purchase on a new phone?**
Open **Settings → Restore purchase**, or tap **Restore purchase** on the Pro screen. Make sure you're
signed in with the same Apple Account you bought Pro with.

**My child asked to buy Pro. Why hasn't it unlocked?**
If "Ask to Buy" is on for your family, the purchase waits for a parent's approval. Pickwheel shows
"Waiting for approval" and unlocks Pro as soon as it's approved, with nothing else to do.

**Can I get a refund?**
Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at
[reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

**Does it work offline?**
Yes, everything works without an internet connection. Only buying or restoring Pro needs to reach the
App Store.

**How do I move my groups to another device?**
With Pro, export each group as a text or CSV file and import it on the other device. Pickwheel has
no account or cloud sync. Your groups also come back if you restore your iPhone from a backup.

**How do I delete my data?**
Delete a group (and its history) from the group's **Edit** page, or clear just its history from the
**History** page. Deleting the app removes everything it stored.

## Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 14 October 2026*

This policy explains how Pickwheel ("the app"), made by [DEVELOPER NAME] ("we", "us"), handles
information. The short version: **we don't collect any data from you.** Everything you enter stays on
your device.

### 1. Information we collect

**None.** Pickwheel has no accounts, no sign-up, no servers of its own, no analytics, no
advertising, no tracking and no third-party data-collection tools. We don't receive your names, groups,
picks, or any information about how you use the app.

On the App Store, Pickwheel's privacy label is **"Data Not Collected"**.

### 2. Information stored on your device

To work, the app stores the following **only on your device**:

- **Groups and names** you create, type, paste or import.
- **Round state:** who has been drawn in the current round, and who is "away today".
- **History** of picks: the name, round, mode and time. The last 500 per group are kept.
- **Settings:** appearance, sound and haptics, the last mode used, team count, dice and number-range
  settings.
- **Pro status:** whether Pro has been purchased, kept in the iOS Keychain so the app works offline.

We can't see any of this. It's removed when you delete it in the app or delete the app. Like other app
data, it may be included in your device's iCloud or computer backups, which are controlled by your
Apple Account settings and Apple's privacy policy.

### 3. In-app purchases

Pro is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment. We never see your name, email
address, Apple Account or payment details. The app asks Apple's StoreKit whether Pro has been
purchased. This is the **only network connection** the app makes, and it goes to Apple. Apple gives
developers sales reports that do not identify you. Apple's handling of your purchase is covered by
[Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

### 4. Files and the clipboard

- **Import:** when you choose to import, the system file picker opens and the app reads only the file
  you select, to add its names to a group.
- **Export:** the app creates a text or CSV file and opens the system share sheet. You decide where it
  goes, whether that's Files, AirDrop, Mail or somewhere else.
- **Clipboard:** the app writes to the clipboard only when you tap **Copy** in Order mode. It never
  reads your clipboard.

### 5. Permissions

Pickwheel does not ask for access to your camera, microphone, photos, contacts, location, calendar,
health data or Face ID. It doesn't use Apple's tracking (ATT) prompt, because it doesn't track you.

### 6. Crash reports

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings (**Settings →
Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements → Share With App Developers**), Apple may send us
anonymous crash reports. These don't contain your groups or names, and you can turn sharing off at any
time.

### 7. Children

Pickwheel is suitable for classrooms and families. Because the app doesn't collect any personal
information from anyone, it doesn't knowingly collect personal information from children. This is
consistent with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the UK and EU GDPR. Names typed
into the app by a teacher or parent stay on that device.

### 8. Your rights

Data protection laws such as the GDPR, the UK GDPR and the CCPA give you rights to access, correct and
delete your personal data. Since we don't hold any of your data, there is nothing for us to access or
delete. You have full control in the app:

- **Correct:** rename any group or name.
- **Delete:** remove names, clear history, delete groups, or delete the app.
- **Export:** with Pro, export any group as a text or CSV file.

### 9. Security

Your data stays on your device and is protected by iOS, including data protection while your iPhone is
locked. Your Pro status is kept in the iOS Keychain.

### 10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top and publish the new version on this page. If
the app ever started collecting data, which we don't plan to do, we would say so here and in the App
Store before it happened.
